Why Quantum Gravity Calculations Explode

Let's walk through this with actual diagrams and numbers so you can see exactly where things go wrong.

First: What's a Feynman Diagram?

It's a picture that represents a mathematical calculation. Two electrons repelling each other looks like this:

Two electrons exchange a virtual photon → they repel

This "tree-level" diagram is easy to calculate. The answer is finite. No problem.

The Problem: Loop Diagrams

But quantum mechanics says you have to include ALL possible ways something can happen. Including weird stuff like this:

The photon briefly becomes an electron-positron pair, then back to a photon

The loop can have ANY momentum. The virtual particles inside can be moving arbitrarily fast. You have to integrate over all possibilities:

Integral = ∫ (something) d⁴k

where k goes from 0 to ∞

Let's Calculate With Numbers

1 Electromagnetism (QED) Loop

For a photon loop in QED, the integral looks roughly like:

∫₀^Λ k³ dk / (k² + m²)²

Let's plug in numbers. Say m = 1 (electron mass in some units), and integrate up to some cutoff Λ:

Cutoff ΛIntegral Value
10~2.3
100~4.6
1000~6.9
10000~9.2
∞→ ∞ (logarithmically)

It diverges, but slowly (logarithmically). The infinity can be absorbed by redefining the electron's charge. This is renormalization — it works!

2 Gravity (Graviton) Loop

For gravity, the coupling involves Newton's constant G and energy. The integral looks like:

∫₀^Λ G² · k³ · k⁴ dk / (k²)² = G² ∫₀^Λ k³ dk

That extra k⁴ in the numerator comes from gravitons coupling to energy (which grows with momentum). Now calculate:

Cutoff ΛIntegral Value
10~2,500
100~25,000,000
1000~250,000,000,000
10000~2.5 × 10¹⁵
∞→ ∞ (quartically = FAST)

It blows up as Λ⁴. Much faster than QED.

3 Why This Breaks Everything

In QED, the logarithmic divergence means you need ONE number (the measured charge) to absorb the infinity. Done.

In gravity, the Λ⁴ divergence means you need a new parameter. But when you go to TWO loops:

Two loops: ∫∫ G⁴ · k⁶ dk dk' → diverges as Λ⁶

That's a WORSE infinity. You need ANOTHER new parameter to absorb it.

Three loops → Λ⁸ → another parameter.
Four loops → Λ¹⁰ → another parameter.
...

Infinite loops → infinite parameters → no predictive power.

Side by Side Comparison

Electromagnetism ✓

Coupling constant: α ≈ 1/137 (dimensionless)

Loop integral: ~ log(Λ)

Fix: Redefine charge and mass (2 parameters)

Result: Finite predictions to 12 decimal places

Gravity ✗

Coupling constant: G ~ 1/Mₚ² (has dimensions!)

Loop integral: ~ Λ⁴, Λ⁶, Λ⁸, ...

Fix: Need infinite new parameters

Result: ∞ — theory breaks

What Does This Look Like Diagrammatically?

Graviton scattering — the loops get worse and worse:

Dashed lines = gravitons. Each loop makes the divergence worse.

The Core Issue: Newton's constant G has units of [length]² or equivalently [1/mass²]. This means at higher energies (smaller distances), gravity coupling gets STRONGER, not weaker. The theory eats itself at high energies.

In QED, higher energies mean weaker effective coupling → loops are tamed.
In gravity, higher energies mean stronger coupling → loops explode.

What Would Fix This?

Something needs to cut off the high-energy behavior. Candidates:

  • String theory: Strings have finite size (~10⁻³⁵ m), so there's a natural cutoff. No true point interactions.
  • Loop quantum gravity: Space itself becomes discrete at small scales. Can't integrate to infinity if space is pixelated.
  • Something else: We don't know yet.

This is the biggest unsolved problem in theoretical physics. Whoever figures it out gets the Nobel Prize and their name in textbooks forever.
