Toroidal ring (real space)

Top-down view. Each dot is a measurement of δT at one of 16 grid points. Color: red = positive, blue = negative.

δT(y) plotted against y

Vertical axis: δT value at that point. Horizontal axis: position around the ring (y from 0 to 2π).
What you're looking at: step through ky = 0, 1, 2, ... 15. For each ky, the 16 dots show the values of cos(ky · y) sampled at the 16 grid points. As ky grows, more wavelengths fit around the ring. At ky = 8 you hit the Nyquist limit — that's the highest frequency 16 points can resolve. Beyond that, aliasing kicks in (try ky = 9 — it looks like ky = 7 in reverse).

Toggle show full δT to see what real turbulence looks like: a superposition of many of these modes. The 16 dots on the ring are the actual numbers GENE stores — and equivalently, the 16 Fourier amplitudes are also what GENE stores. They're the same data.
