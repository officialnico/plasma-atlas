Quantum Chromodynamics · Color Confinement

Every way to bind quarks & gluons

Quarks carry a property called color charge — red, green, or blue (and anti­colors for antiquarks). Nature only lets particles exist if their colors add up to white. That single rule produces every combination below. Gluons are the glue: they carry color too, and constantly swap it between quarks. Pick a state to watch it bind.

Reading the chamber

Glowing orbs are quarks; split orbs are gluons (they carry a color + an anticolor). The wavy links are flux tubes — the strong force, which gets stronger with distance, so quarks can never escape alone. Pulses traveling the tubes are gluons handing color between quarks while keeping the total white.
