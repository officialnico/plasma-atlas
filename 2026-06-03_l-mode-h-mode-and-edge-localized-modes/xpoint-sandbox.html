Move the X-Point with Coil Currents

Six poloidal-field coils (PF1–PF6) and the plasma current. Each coil is a ring of current; the app sums their magnetic flux ψ(R,Z), then finds where the poloidal field cancels to zero — the X-point. Drag a coil's current and watch the null (and the whole boundary) move. Positive current is red, negative blue.

This is a current-filament superposition model (a teaching tool), not a self-consistent Grad–Shafranov solve — the plasma shape doesn't relax to the field. But the way coil currents create and steer the null is faithful: the X-point sits wherever a coil's poloidal field exactly opposes the plasma's.
