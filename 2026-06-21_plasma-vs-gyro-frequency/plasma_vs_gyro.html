Plasma frequency vs gyromotion

Both are "fast small scales" a simulation must resolve — but they're completely different motions.

Plasma frequencyelectrostatic · back-and-forth
f ≈ 1.0

Electrons (blue) get nudged sideways off the fixed ion lattice (orange). The exposed charge pulls them back, they overshoot, and oscillate. Denser plasma → stiffer restoring → faster.

Gyromotionmagnetic · circular
f ≈ 1.0

A charged particle spirals around a magnetic field line (⊙ = field out of the page). Radius r = gyroradius, laps per second = gyrofrequency. Stronger B → faster and tighter.
