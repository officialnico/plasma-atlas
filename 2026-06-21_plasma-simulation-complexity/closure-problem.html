the closure problem

Each equation needs the next one up.

Take velocity moments of the Vlasov equation and you get a ladder of fluid equations. Every rung's rate of change depends on the rung above it — density needs velocity, velocity needs pressure, pressure needs heat flux, on and on. There is no top rung, so a finite fluid model can only exist if you cut the ladder by hand and invent a value for the dangling term. That invented value is a closure.
