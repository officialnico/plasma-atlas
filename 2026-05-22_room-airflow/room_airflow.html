The play: fan at the sun-side window pointed out creates negative pressure. Hot air gets pushed out, cooler-ish air gets pulled in through the door on the opposite side and drawn diagonally across the seating cluster. Curtain on the sun-side glass kills radiant heat without blocking the exhaust path at the fan.
