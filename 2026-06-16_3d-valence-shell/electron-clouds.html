Electron Clouds

probability density · hydrogenic orbitals

An electron has no fixed path. Each fuzzy cloud is where it’s likely to be — sampled from |ψ|². A shell isn’t one ring: it’s several orbitals layered in space, each holding up to 2 electrons. The dark gaps are nodes, where the electron never appears.
