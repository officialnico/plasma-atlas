Where the electron actually is

The shaded band is the region where the 1s electron almost always lives. Notice the 2s curve (and even 3s) has a small bump inside that band — those outer-shell electrons spend part of their time underneath the inner shell. That’s penetration: there’s no clean boundary, only overlapping odds. On average each shell sits farther out (the big peaks march rightward), but at any instant a higher-shell electron can be found closer in than a lower-shell one.
