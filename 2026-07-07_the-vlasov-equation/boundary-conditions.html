Boundary conditions · x = ±5 walls

Clip doesn't build a wall. These do.

Same particles, all drifting at constant velocity toward the walls at x = ±5. Flip the mode and watch what happens when they reach the edge. Only two of these actually keep particles in — clip just lets them sail off.

None / clip — the red walls do nothing; particles pass right through and drift off forever. This is why you saw no bounce-back: clip only fixes the bin lookup, never the particle.

Reflecting — a real wall. Particle reaches ±5, position folds back in, velocity reverses. This is the "pushed back into x = 5" you would expect.

Periodic — no wall, but nothing escapes: leave the right edge, come back on the left. This is the standard choice for the two-stream / instability runs.
