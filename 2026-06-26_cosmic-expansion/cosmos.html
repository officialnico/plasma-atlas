Visualizer 02 — The shape of the history

Decelerate, then accelerate

Plotting the expansion rate across all of cosmic time reveals a clean three-act structure. Gravity braked the early universe; then, about 5 billion years ago, dark energy overtook matter and the brake became an accelerator. The dot tracks your position from the slider above.

Visualizer 03 — Why there is no centre

v = H · r, from anywhere

The expansion law is the one pattern that looks identical from every point. The left panel centres on us; the right re-centres on a distant galaxy. Same field, same rule — each observer sees everything fleeing them, and neither is the true middle. Arrows beyond the red ring recede faster than light.

A teaching toy, not a simulation engine. The curves are schematic — qualitatively faithful to ΛCDM (radiation → matter → dark-energy eras, deceleration giving way to acceleration ~5 Gyr ago), with timings and rates stylised for clarity rather than fit to data. H₀ ≈ 70 km/s/Mpc; the deeper "why it accelerates" remains the largest open question in physics.
