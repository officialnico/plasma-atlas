DNA Strain Gyrase Cipro

Why it's safe for you: human cells use structurally different topoisomerase enzymes, so cipro barely binds them — it targets the bacterial version and mostly leaves yours alone. That selectivity is the whole reason it's a drug and not a poison. Resistance happens when a tiny mutation reshapes the bacterial enzyme's binding pocket so cipro can't latch on — common now in Campylobacter across SE Asia.
