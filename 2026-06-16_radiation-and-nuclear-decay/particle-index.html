The parameters tying them together: the species couple through a handful of dimensionless numbers — ρ* = ρ_i/a (gyro-Bohm scaling, δf validity), β (electromagnetic effects, KBM onset), ν* (collisionality regime), and the mass ratio m_i/m_e (the multiscale cost). The instability each species drives — ITG (main ions), TEM·ETG·MTM (electrons), KBM (finite-β), TAE·EPM (alphas) — is really a map of which gradient in which species the turbulence feeds on.

03 / THE MIRRORAntimatter

Every fermion has a distinct antiparticle — opposite charge, same mass.

The electron's is the positron, the proton's the antiproton, and each quark, neutrino, muon, and tau has its twin. Photon, Z, Higgs, and the gluon family are their own antiparticles; W⁺ and W⁻ are each other's. For your work it barely matters: positrons appear only fleetingly in β⁺-driven fusion (e.g. the Sun's p–p chain, or PET tracers), annihilating almost immediately into photons. No antimatter species lives in a confinement plasma.

Quarks bound by gluons. The list is open-ended — but for fusion only the two lightest baryons matter, and they're the building blocks of every species in section 02.

Only the proton and neutron are fusion-relevant — assembled into D, T, ³He and α. The heavier hyperons carry strange/charm quarks, decay in nanoseconds, and never appear in a plasma.

Mesons

quark + antiquark (qq̄) · bosons

No fusion or plasma role — all unstable, all gone in well under a microsecond. Listed for completeness of the particle zoo.

The ladder upward is your real domain: nucleons (p, n) bind into nuclei — deuteron, triton, ³He, the α — and those, plus the electron, are exactly the section-02 species. Fusion is the rearrangement of these nuclei; gyrokinetics is the turbulent transport of them while they're hot enough to do it.

THE PARTICLE INDEX · FUSION & GYROKINETICS EDITION

The whole of fundamental physics offers 17 elementary particles — but your gyrokinetic code evolves perhaps four to six of their bound combinations: kinetic electrons, deuterium and tritium main ions, an energetic alpha population, and a trace impurity or two. The neutron carries off most of the energy and never appears in your equations. Everything else on this page is context for that short, decisive list.
