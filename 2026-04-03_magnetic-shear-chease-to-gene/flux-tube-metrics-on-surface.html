FLUX TUBE — METRIC-TRANSFORMED CELLS ON THE SURFACE

The purple parallelograms are GENE's grid cells placed on the actual flux surface. Each cell is transformed by the local metric values at that poloidal position.

At the outboard side (right), g^xy ≈ 0, so cells are upright rectangles. Moving toward the inboard side (left), g^xy grows and tilts them into parallelograms. The further from the outboard midplane, the more tilted. That tilt is shear distorting the grid.

Set shear to 0 — all cells become upright. Crank it to 3 — cells near the top/bottom/inboard get heavily skewed. That's exactly what the metric coefficients encode.

OUTBOARD vs TOP vs INBOARD — SAME GENE CELL, DIFFERENT METRICS

Same cell in GENE's coordinates (green square), but the metrics at each position produce a different real shape (purple).

Outboard (z=0): g^xy=0, cell is nearly square. This is the only place GENE's coordinates match reality.

Top (z=π/2): g^xy has grown, cell is tilted. Shear is stretching things.

Inboard (z=π): g^xy is at maximum, cell is heavily skewed. Plus g^yy has grown so it's taller. The metrics capture all of this distortion.
