Reading the map: The horizontal axis is z — position along the field line from outboard (center) through top, inboard, bottom, back to outboard. The vertical axis is the radial layers — GENE's grid across the tube width.

Each cell is drawn as its real shape after metrics, not as a uniform square. In the center column (z≈0, outboard) the cells are upright. Moving toward the edges (z=±π, inboard) they tilt from shear. The cell color shows curvature: red = bad, green = good.

Set shear to 0 — all cells become identical upright rectangles. Crank to 3 — the edges tilt dramatically. That's the whole story of what the metrics encode.
