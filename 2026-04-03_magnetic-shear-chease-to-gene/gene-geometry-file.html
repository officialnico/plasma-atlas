That's the entire geometry file. Three single numbers at the top, six 1D arrays below. Each array has the same number of points (typically 128 or 256) sampled along z from outboard → top → inboard → bottom → back. Hover across any plot and the mini cross-section shows exactly where that z-point sits on the flux surface. GENE reads this once at startup, then runs the whole turbulence simulation using just these curves.
