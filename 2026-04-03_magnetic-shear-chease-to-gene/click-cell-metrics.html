The tube has multiple radial layers — these represent GENE's radial grid points across the thin tube width. Each layer is a ring of cells. The metrics are the same for all radial layers at a given poloidal position (same z), but the shear tilt (g^xy) shifts each layer slightly differently because they're on slightly different flux surfaces.

More layers = GENE resolves finer radial structure in the turbulence. More poloidal cells = finer resolution along the field line.
