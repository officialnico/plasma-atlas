The Deck of Cards

SHEAR = PUSHING A DECK SIDEWAYSSAME SLIDER

A deck of cards standing upright = zero shear. Push the top sideways = shear. Each card tilts, but they're still stacked flush — no card overlaps another. That's exactly what happens to the radial layers in the flux tube. The amount of push increases with z (distance from the outboard midplane).
