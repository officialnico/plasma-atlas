What you're seeing

Each colored dot is a field line marker on a different flux surface. They all move toroidally (around the big ring of the tokamak) at the same speed — but their poloidal rotation rate differs because each surface has a different q.

The inner surface has low q ≈ 1, so its field line completes a full poloidal loop quickly. The outer surface has high q, so it barely moves poloidally in the same time. Shear controls how big this difference is — crank it up and watch them separate.

When ŝ = 0, all surfaces twist at the same rate (no shear). As you increase ŝ, the outer surfaces lag behind more and more — that differential twisting is what tears apart turbulent eddies.
