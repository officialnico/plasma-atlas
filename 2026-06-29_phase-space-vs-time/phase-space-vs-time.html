The Clock / The Map

Every system below is animated once. On the left it's plotted the ordinary way — a quantity ticking along against time, the way an oscilloscope or a chart draws it. On the right is the same motion in phase space: no time axis at all, just the state plotted against itself. The glowing point is a single shared instant. Watch how a tangled, repeating, or chaotic time trace becomes a clean shape once you stop plotting against the clock.
