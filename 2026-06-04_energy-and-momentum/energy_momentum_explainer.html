velocity → energy & momentum

Drag the arrow to set a velocity. The amber box is that velocity squared — its side is the arrow’s length, its area is v². Press play to watch each step build.

1.0
velocity, speed, momentum — lengths (linear in v) v² and energy — areas (square of v)
1
the velocity, split into components
vx = 3.0,  vy = 4.0
2
square each — this is the v² step
3.0² = 9.0,  4.0² = 16.0
3
add the squares (Pythagoras) → v²
9.0 + 16.0 = 25.0
4
speed is the square root
√25.0 = 5.0
5
momentum = mass × speed
1.0 × 5.0 = 5.0
6
energy = ½ × mass × v²
½ × 1.0 × 25.0 = 12.5

Where the squaring is: the amber box. Its side is the arrow’s length (the speed), so its area is the speed squared — that’s v², exactly the 9 + 16 = 25 from steps 2–3. Drag the arrow and watch the box’s area grow as the square of its side, while the blue momentum bar (just the length) grows in proportion. Energy is half that area, times mass; momentum is the length, times mass. Steps 1–4 never touch the mass — it only enters at steps 5 and 6 as a plain multiplier.
