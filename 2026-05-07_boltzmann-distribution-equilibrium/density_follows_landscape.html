Notice how the density peak (the tallest red bar at the bottom) always sits exactly under that dashed line — not at x = 0, not at the center, not at any fixed location. It tracks U wherever U goes. Try the "ramp" shape — U decreases monotonically from right to left, so density peaks at the far left and decays exponentially toward the right. Same formula, totally different shape, because the landscape is different. Try "hill" — the highest U is in the middle, so density is lowest in the middle and piles up at both edges. The formula doesn't care about geometry. It just says: be where U is small.
