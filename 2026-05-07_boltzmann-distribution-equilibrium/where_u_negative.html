— scene 1: single positive ion A lone positive ion in a plasma. The electron's potential energy U = q·φ = (−e)·φ. Near the ion, φ is positive (it's a positive charge), so U is negative — the entire blue region around the ion. Far from the ion, U → 0 (the yellow band) — there's nothing pulling on the electron. The closer you get to the ion, the deeper U goes negative. This is why electrons cluster around positive ions.
