Adjust the shaking (temperature) to see how cold landscapes give sharp pile-ups and hot landscapes give flat distributions. This is exactly what the Boltzmann formula describes.

— what to look for Drag the shaking slider all the way down and watch the marbles freeze into the lowest spot. That's a cold system — particles huddle in the deepest valley. Drag it all the way up and watch them spread out almost evenly — that's a hot system, where shaking dominates the landscape. The histogram at the bottom of the scene shows the actual density distribution, with the theoretical Boltzmann prediction (red dashed line) overlaid. Try the two valleys landscape to see how marbles split between them based on which is deeper — that's exactly how electrons distribute themselves around different ions in a plasma.
